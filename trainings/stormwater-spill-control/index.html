Stormwater Awareness and Spill Control Training

Purpose

This training helps you understand how to prevent spills, leaks, oils, coolants, chemicals, and contaminated water from reaching storm drains, floor drains, outside drains, soil, or retention ponds.

Why does this matter? Anything that enters a storm drain may reach the environment without treatment. Protecting stormwater is everyone's responsibility.

Key Awareness Points

  • Storm drains are for rainwater only
  • Do not dump liquids into any drain
  • Keep all containers closed when not in use
  • Report leaking containers immediately
  • Keep outdoor areas clean and free of spills

What You Must Do

  • Watch for leaks, spills, and unusual liquids
  • Keep liquids away from all drains
  • Never dump anything into a storm drain
  • Report all spills, leaks, and unusual liquids immediately

Remember: Storm drains are for rainwater only. Report spills, leaks, and unusual liquids immediately.
