# Instructions for Building Training Files

## Goal

Create simple, professional, mobile-friendly EHS training pages that can be hosted, reviewed, and accessed by QR code.

## Standard Training Page Format

Each training should include:

1. Training title
2. 2. Purpose
   3. 3. Who needs this training
      4. 4. Key requirements
         5. 5. What employees need to do
            6. 6. What supervisors need to verify
               7. 7. Short quiz
                  8. 8. Pass/fail result
                     9. 9. Completion instruction
                       
                        10. ## Writing Style
                       
                        11. Use plain language for shop-floor employees.
                       
                        12. Keep the wording:
                        13. - Short
                            - - Direct
                              - - Easy to understand
                                - - Site-specific
                                  - - Accurate to the procedure
                                   
                                    - Avoid:
                                    - - Overly legal wording
                                      - - Long paragraphs
                                        - - Unverified OSHA or EPA claims
                                          - - Guessing at requirements
                                            - - Adding rules that are not approved
                                             
                                              - ## AI Instructions
                                             
                                              - When using AI to create a training page, provide:
                                             
                                              - - Training topic
                                                - - Source procedure or notes
                                                  - - Required rules
                                                    - - Quiz questions and correct answers
                                                      - - Any images to include
                                                        - - Audience
                                                          - - Required pass score
                                                            - - Any site-specific wording
                                                             
                                                              - AI should generate:
                                                             
                                                              - - A mobile-friendly HTML file
                                                                - - Clean layout
                                                                  - - Short sections
                                                                    - - Interactive quiz
                                                                      - - Score result
                                                                        - - Review note at the bottom
                                                                          - - No employee personal information
                                                                           
                                                                            - ## Required Review Before Use
                                                                           
                                                                            - Before publishing or sharing, review:
                                                                           
                                                                            - - Procedure references
                                                                              - - Correct answers
                                                                                - - Site-specific requirements
                                                                                  - - Spelling
                                                                                    - - Mobile layout
                                                                                      - - QR code link
                                                                                        - - Whether the content is approved for employee use
                                                                                         
                                                                                          - ## Training Record Warning
                                                                                         
                                                                                          - This repository does not automatically prove employee training completion.
                                                                                         
                                                                                          - Use the approved company system for official training records unless leadership approves another process.
